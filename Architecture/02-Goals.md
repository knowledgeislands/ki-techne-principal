# Goals

## Purpose

This chapter defines the outcomes that guide the development of Techne and the engineering practice it describes.

Goals provide a stable basis for prioritisation, architectural review, and the evaluation of proposed changes.

## Establish a Shared Engineering Discipline

Provide a common engineering language for the Knowledge Islands ecosystem.

Contributors should be able to understand the system's responsibilities, interfaces, operating assumptions, and decision-making practices without relying on unwritten context.

## Make AI-Native Engineering Operable

Define how AI capabilities are introduced, routed, evaluated, and operated as part of the engineering platform.

The practice must account for both probabilistic agent behaviour and deterministic engineering operations, with clear boundaries between their responsibilities.

## Preserve Governed Knowledge

Treat knowledge as an engineering asset with provenance, context, lifecycle, and access considerations.

Implementation patterns should support knowledge that can be discovered, reviewed, reused, and evolved without weakening the governance model of Knowledge Islands.

## Support Deliberate Execution Choices

Provide a provider-neutral framework for selecting local, managed, elastic, or dedicated execution targets.

Selection should be explicit about capability, latency, privacy, cost, context length, locality, and availability rather than being driven solely by a default provider or runtime.

## Enable Incremental Evolution

Make it possible to improve the platform in small, reviewable steps.

Architecture, operating guidance, technology evaluations, decisions, and diagrams should remain coherent as the platform changes.

## Remain Useful in Practice

Produce documentation that informs engineering work, not merely descriptions of it.

Each chapter should identify its rationale, constraints, trade-offs, and relationships to the material required to apply it.
