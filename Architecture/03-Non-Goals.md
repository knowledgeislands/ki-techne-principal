# Non-Goals

## Purpose

Techne is a focused engineering discipline, not a universal reference for all technology work.

These boundaries preserve clarity of purpose and help contributors distinguish an architectural concern from a topic that belongs elsewhere.

## Replace Arcadia

Techne does not redefine the philosophy, purpose, or broader worldview of Knowledge Islands.

It applies that context to engineering practice and links to Arcadia when philosophical guidance is required.

## Prescribe a Single Technology Stack

Techne does not treat current technologies as permanent requirements by default.

It documents their purpose, constraints, and lifecycle position, while retaining the ability to replace them when a better fit emerges.

## Be a Product Specification

Techne does not provide complete feature requirements, interaction design, or delivery plans for every product or service.

Those artefacts should reference relevant Techne guidance when they make architectural or operational commitments.

## Substitute for Component Documentation

Techne does not duplicate installation instructions, API references, runbooks, or implementation details maintained by a component's own repository.

It records the architectural role, integration pattern, and operational consequences of a component, and links to its authoritative documentation where appropriate.

## Treat AI Output as Authority

Techne does not regard agent or model output as an architectural decision, a source of truth, or a substitute for review.

AI-assisted work remains subject to appropriate engineering controls, evidence, and accountable decision-making.

## Freeze the Architecture

Techne does not attempt to produce a final architecture before sufficient evidence exists.

Uncertainty is recorded explicitly and revisited through decisions, evaluations, and roadmap work.
