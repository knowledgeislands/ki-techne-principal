# Enactment Process

Techne uses the Knowledge Islands Enactment Process for substantive changes to canonical knowledge.

Work begins in [[Streams]] as a proposal. The proposal records its inputs, outputs, checklist, open questions, and governance. When the proposal is ready and approved, its checklist may change canonical knowledge in [[Admin]], [[Pillars]], or [[Resources]].

After rollout, the change is reviewed against its stated outputs and verification evidence. Settled knowledge remains in its canonical zone; the completed proposal is removed once its working value has ended.

The canonical process definition is governed by `ki-kb-streams`.
